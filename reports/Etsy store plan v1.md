## 5. The foil designs

- **No US dropshipper makes real hot foil personalised per order.** StationeryHQ does real hot foil only on cards with an $80 die per design; TOG.ink does raised digital foil only in packs of 5, which loses money on a one-off.
- **Closest US route: Prodigi's US lab, metallic foil stock** (white areas print as gold or silver; no rose gold; not raised). 72-hour production, white-label, Etsy integration, 50% off the first sample. Must be priced at $49.95+ to clear ~36%.
- **Decision rule:** order the Prodigi gold and silver samples and a Printkeg metallic sample to a US address, compare side by side with the UK product. If they pass, move US foil listings to Prodigi and the ships-from becomes genuinely US. If they fail, UK foil listings show UK origin on Etsy, and the UK-made range goes to UK channels (§7).

## 6. Listing standard (applies to every new listing)

Etsy-stated (2025–26): titles under 15 words, noun first, occasions only if essential; fill all attributes and the correct category; up to 20 photos and a 5–15 second video; shipping under $6 for US domestic or free shipping on $35+ orders in shops with the free-shipping guarantee; use the Search Visibility page to fix flagged listings; Gift Mode and AI search reward clear recipient/occasion signals. Etsy's Q3 2026 letter says search is weighting quality signals (conversion, reviews, service) more heavily and personalisation is adding growth.

Third-party practice (eRank, Marmalead): 13 tags with long-tail phrases that mirror buyer searches; do not edit listings that are already selling; launch in batches; protect Star Seller (dispatch on time, message response, reviews).

Standard per listing: a personalisation field with a worked example; three mockups in a consistent house style (room scene, close-up, size guide) plus the video; the price ladder as variations (size × format) so one listing carries entry to framed; processing time stated honestly for each partner.

## 8. US presence

- Current shops stay UK-registered; their country cannot be changed.
- A new US-based shop needs a US entity of your own (non-resident LLC: Wyoming ~$100 to form, ~$60/yr + ~$125/yr agent; EIN; Mercury/Relay/Wise account; annual Form 5472 + pro forma 1120, $25k penalty if missed; ~$300–1,200/yr US side). HMRC treats a US LLC as a company and one managed from the UK may be UK-resident for corporation tax; an accountant must confirm. It mainly buys US banking and currency; marketplaces already collect US sales tax.
- **Do not use a US bank account that is not in your name or your own company's name.** It breaks Etsy's name-matching rule and can trigger holds or suspension that reach the live shops.
- Decision: no US entity until Lane A revenue justifies the compliance, and no new shop until Etsy's written answer on the cancelled shop.
