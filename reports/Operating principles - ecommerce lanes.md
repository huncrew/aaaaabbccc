# Operating principles for the two lanes (living list, v1, 3 Nov 2026)

Each line is a rule we hold ourselves to, with the evidence it came from. Add to it when a test teaches us something; never delete a line, strike it through with the date and the reason.

## Products and pricing
1. **Cheapest variant first, every line.** A digital file or small print at the market median is the door; canvas, framed and signs carry the margin. Evidence: star-map leaders anchor $5–23 entry; our own record, the pet-portrait run worked with a cheap variant and the foil range without one hurt the shops.
2. **Entry price at the US market median, never above it.** The median is what the buyer compares against in search. Evidence: RankHero medians by keyword; OrbitalPerspective ranking at $23 inside 2 years.
3. **No new line without a demand number.** Monthly searches, listings per search, seasonality index, median price, and a partner cost before the first listing. Lines that cannot carry 65+ orders a month on their own numbers are fillers, not lines.
4. **No design labour per order on volume lines.** Templates (date, place, names) render by machine; labour lines (portraits) are priced for it or capped.
5. **No supplier, no line.** Foil was listed before a US producer existed. Every format has a named partner with a verified price and a sample in hand before it goes live.
6. **Year-round first.** Lines with a seasonality index under 1.5 are core; seasonal lines are campaigns with a start and an end date.

## Listings and search
7. **Copy the leaders, systematically.** Tag sets, price ladders and formats come from the benchmark shops' live listings, pulled by API and ranked by frequency, not from instinct.
8. **Title under 15 words, noun first, no occasion stuffing; 13 tags under 20 characters, long-tail, one idea each.** Etsy's 2025 guidance plus eRank/Marmalead practice.
9. **One listing per search door.** Style, colour and occasion variants are separate listings; sizes and formats are variations inside a listing. More doors, same brand.
10. **Do not edit a listing that is selling.** Duplicate it and change the copy.
11. **Ads only on listings already converting above 2%, capped at 5% of revenue.** Offsite Ads orders cost 12 points of margin; price the ladder knowing some orders will be those.

## Images
12. **First photo sells the product, not the mockup.** The product large, in a room, lit like a photograph, personalisation legible. The leaders' first photos are lifestyle, not flat lays.
13. **Three images minimum, same house style per shop:** room scene, close-up with the personalisation readable, size guide showing every format. Add the 5–15 second video.
14. **Show the real result.** For proof-based lines, the proof-to-print sequence is a photo. Reviews with photos outrank the mockup.
15. **Square 2000px, no text in the first image except the personalisation itself.**

## Operations
16. **Processing time stated honestly and short:** digital instant; prints 3–7 days; proof lines 1–2 days to proof.
17. **Free US shipping over $35; under-$6 shipping on anything cheaper.** Etsy prioritises it in US search.
18. **Production partners disclosed on every listing; ships-from accurate on every listing; both shops listed in each other's profile.**
19. **Weekly review on one sheet:** visits, conversion, orders, order value, margin after fees, ad spend, dispatch on time, reviews. Monday, 60 minutes, decisions only.
20. **Kill rule:** under 25% margin for two months, or under 1% conversion after 60 days and 1,000 views. Double rule: over 2% conversion, double the listings.

## Trade lane
21. **No stock without five orders. No second order without five reorders inside 13 weeks. £40k stock cap.**
22. **No listing without the test report.** The surge-protection standard, earth-rod standard, non-spillable letter, joint-kit standard, in hand from the supplier, not claimed.
23. **Batteries Regulations registration before the first battery sale.**
24. **Trade price = UK list × 0.90; marketplace = × 0.95; walk away under 30% landed margin except on captive lines.**
25. **Three suppliers per SKU, one UK fallback always open.**

## Data and tools
26. **Platform data through the platform's API or the seller's own exports; third-party tools for the rest; blocked pages are recorded, not circumvented.**
27. **Every number in a plan has a source URL and a date.** Estimates are flagged as estimates.
28. **Bulk upload by file wherever a platform allows it; API where it does not.** Amazon and eBay accept CSV uploads; Etsy does not create new listings from CSV, so Etsy goes through the API or a print partner's push.
