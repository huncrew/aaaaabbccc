# AWS consulting partner route vs AI app / vertical SaaS route — base rates and economics (as of 28 September 2026)

Scope note: UK figures wherever available; USD/global figures are flagged. Every number is dated and traced to a URL. Where a figure comes only from a secondary aggregator or blog, it is flagged as such. Nothing below is estimated from memory; where evidence could not be found it is listed under Gaps.

Founder profile assumed throughout: SC-cleared AWS / Amazon Connect / agentic-AI solutions architect, strong face-to-face seller, 0/6 solo builds finished, no demand-generation skill, limited daily energy (Crohn's), £50–110k risk capital.

## Key question 1 — Route A: If 100 experienced contractors each start an AWS/AI consultancy in 2027 selling face to face, how many reach £70k+ owner profit within 24 months, how many reach £150k+ EBITDA with a delivery team by 2030, and what would that firm sell for?

### Takeaway
The programme rails are cheap and well documented (APN fee US$2,500/yr; Select tier needs 3 launched deals and 2+2 certified people; Advanced needs 20 launched deals and 6 technical certs), but the population data are sobering: 97% of UK computer consultancies have 0–9 employees, only ~3% turn over more than £1m, only ~2.5% employ 10+ people, and five-year survival for new information-and-communication businesses is 37%. Contractor day rates for AWS solution architects have fallen 17% year on year to a £500 median (Sept 2026), while G-Cloud consultancy rate cards still list £700–£1,550 per day, so the arbitrage exists but must be sold. Small UK IT-services firms with £300–750k EBITDA sell at roughly 2.5–4.5x EBITDA; owner-dependent firms are discounted or earn-out-structured.

### Inferences
- Day-rate arbitrage: a contractor engineer at the £500–625/day contract median (IT Jobs Watch, Sept 2026) billed at G-Cloud Level 4–5 rates of £728–£1,200/day implies a gross margin of roughly 30–55% per billed day before utilisation loss, sales cost and bench time; at the SPI benchmark of 68.9% utilisation and 9.8% average EBITDA, most of that gross margin is consumed by overhead unless the founder is also billing.
- £70k owner profit inside 24 months is essentially "one contractor-equivalent of billed days" (140 days × £500 = £70k), so a face-to-face seller who can bill personally has a plausible path; the ONS 2-year survival of 76.7% for new I&C firms is the outer bound, and the 91.8% of computer consultancies turning over under £250k (2019) indicates most never exceed a single-person income.
- £150k+ EBITDA with a delivery team by 2030 requires turnover roughly above £1m at 10–15% margins; only 3.0% of UK computer consultancies exceed £1m turnover and only 2.5% employ 10+ people (ONS 2019), and the cohort has to survive 3+ years (56% for 2019 I&C births). A base rate in the low single digits per 100 is consistent with these figures, before adjusting for the founder's selling strength and SC clearance.
- Sale value: £150k EBITDA is below the smallest band EFC quotes (£300k+ at 2.5–4.5x), so a realistic outcome is either an unsellable lifestyle firm or a low-single-digit multiple with earn-out (e.g. £150k × 2.5–3.5x ≈ £375k–£525k), discounted further if the founder holds all client relationships. Govtech's 16.5x EBITDA / 3.7x revenue (my calculation from the RNS: £13m ÷ £0.79m; £13m ÷ £3.5m) shows what recurring public-sector SaaS with 39 staff and ~50 councils fetches — a product, not a consultancy, outcome.
- UK AWS partner deals (Ubertas, Rebura) confirm strategic buyers exist for Advanced-tier UK boutiques, but neither disclosed price, revenue or headcount, and their filed accounts are image PDFs that could not be text-extracted here.

## Key question 2 — Route B: If 100 technical founders with a second person build a vertical AI app in 2027 with £50k, how many reach £100k ARR by 2029, and what are the exit odds and sizes?

### Takeaway
Indie/bootstrapped SaaS data say most products stall under US$1k MRR (28% in MicroConf's 2023 survey of 469 founders; ~70% in a 1,000-product scan) and that even among companies already billing customers only 13.4% reach US$1M ARR within three years (ChartMogul, 6,525 companies, Oct 2025). AI-app gross margins are structurally lower (45% in 2025, projected 53% in 2026 per ICONIQ) than classic SaaS (~71% average on Acquire.com). Small SaaS exits clear at a median 3.9x profit (Acquire.com, 2024 and 2025), so a £100k-ARR app at 50% margin is a low-six-figure sale at best. Public-sector selling adds long cycles (Gartner: 22 months average for government tech purchases) and the AI-contract money is concentrated in large suppliers (Microsoft, Palantir, KPMG), although a £900m NHS AI framework open to SMEs and rising SME share of local-government spend (34%) are real tailwinds.

### Inferences
- US$100k ARR ≈ US$8.3k MRR. In MicroConf's 2023 sample, 28% were below US$1k MRR and 65% had ≤10 customers; in the 1,000-product scan 88% were below US$5k MRR. Those are survivor samples of products that already launched. For a 2027 cohort of 100 two-person teams, reaching £100k ARR by end-2029 (≈30 months) is therefore a minority outcome; ChartMogul's 13.4% reaching US$1M ARR within three years (a 10x higher bar, but conditional on already billing) and the 60% three-year survival of 2021-born UK I&C firms bracket the plausible range. A defensible planning number is on the order of 10–25 of 100 reaching £100k ARR, with the higher end only if the founder truly pre-sells to warm public-sector or contact-centre relationships (Camuffo's RCTs show disciplined hypothesis-testing raises termination of bad ideas and revenue, not that warm networks guarantee revenue).
- Exit sizing at £100k ARR: at Acquire.com's 3.9x median profit multiple and an AI-app gross margin of ~50%, a £100k-ARR product with, say, £40–50k profit is a ~£150–200k sale; at 1–3x revenue it is £100–300k. Netcall's £13m for £3.4m ARR shows the ceiling requires ~50 public-sector logos and £3m+ ARR.
- Build cost: at £550–575/day, 60–100 contracted developer days (a common 3–5 month MVP) is £33–58k, i.e. most of a £50k budget before AWS run costs (AgentCore run costs at pilot scale are low four figures per month at most on the published rates, plus model tokens). The US$50K Amazon Connect MDF and US$25K POC funding (Route A rails) only pay if the firm is an AWS partner delivering to an AWS customer, not for building its own product.
- Public-sector timeline: with 6–36 month cycles (GovNet) and a 22-month average tech buying cycle (Gartner), a 2027 start realistically produces first paid production contracts in 2028–29 unless the first sale is a sub-threshold direct award (below £135,018 central / £207,720 other, incl. VAT) to a buyer the founder already knows.

## Key question 4 — Hours per week, stress and burnout for founders of early-stage services vs product firms, and evidence on part-time (3-day) founding

### Takeaway
Startup-founder surveys show 67% working over 50 hours a week and 54% reporting burnout (Sifted 2025, 156 European founders), and indie-SaaS data show full-time founders grow 30% faster than part-time ones (MicroConf 2023), but the best causal evidence (Raffiee & Feng 2014) finds that hybrid/part-time entry followed by later full-time commitment produces higher survival than jumping straight in. No study directly compares hours or burnout for consultancy founders versus product founders.

### Cited Findings
- Sifted founder mental-health survey 2025 (156 founders, Europe): 54% experienced burnout in the past 12 months (same as prior year); 67% work over 50 hours a week; 49% say workload rose in the last year; 46% rate their mental health "bad" or "very bad" (45% in 2024); 75% anxiety; 39% considering quitting within a year (49% in 2024); 56% receive no mental-health support from investors — [Sifted, 2025](https://sifted.eu/articles/founders-mental-health-2025); 2024 wave — [Sifted, 2024](https://sifted.eu/articles/founder-mental-health-2024)
- MicroConf State of Independent SaaS (469 founders, late 2023): full-time founders grow 30% faster than part-time — [MicroConf](https://microconf.com/founders/0-10k-arr) (Freemius restates this as "2x" — conflict noted) — [Freemius](https://freemius.com/blog/state-of-micro-saas-2025/)
- Raffiee & Feng (AMJ 2014; NLSY79): hybrid entrepreneurs who later go full-time have "much higher survival rates" than direct full-time entrants; risk-averse and less confident individuals prefer hybrid entry; "risk aversion influences the process of how an individual decides to start a business (i.e., full-time versus hybrid), not necessarily whether" — [TIM Review](https://timreview.ca/article/1063); [AMJ](https://journals.aom.org/doi/abs/10.5465/amj.2012.0522)
- Kuske, Schulz & Schwens (Entrepreneurship Theory and Practice, 2024/2025; longitudinal data): hybrid entrepreneurs' well-being benefits from the transition to full-time are weakened by role demands outside entrepreneurship (e.g. caring responsibilities), with gender differences — [ETP article](https://journals.sagepub.com/doi/full/10.1177/10422587241288108)
- SPI 2025 benchmark: professional-services billable utilisation 68.9% (implies ~1,400 billable hours per consultant-year on a 2,000-hour base) — [Saibon utilisation benchmark](https://www.saibongroup.com/blogs/consultant-utilization-rate-benchmark)
- US entrepreneur well-being study (Fortune op-ed, 12 Sept 2025): authors report high rates of burnout, anxiety and depression among American entrepreneurs (figures not extracted here) — [Fortune](https://fortune.com/2025/09/12/we-studied-entrepreneurs-burnout-anxious-depressed-wellbeing/)

### Inferences
- For a founder with limited daily energy, the consultancy route's hours are bounded by billable capacity (each £ of owner profit ≈ one billed day at £500–625), whereas product founders in the Sifted sample report >50-hour weeks for uncertain revenue. A 3-day founding pattern is supported by Raffiee & Feng's survival evidence but MicroConf's growth data say it costs ~30% growth.

### Gaps
- No study found comparing weekly hours or burnout between founders of services firms and product firms.
- No UK data on hours worked by boutique consultancy owners (Consultancy BenchPress 2025/26 is gated to participants/members; MCA surveys cover larger firms).
- No quantified effect size for Raffiee & Feng's survival advantage was retrievable (paywalled); only the direction of effect is confirmed by secondary summaries.
