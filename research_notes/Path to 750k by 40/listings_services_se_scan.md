# Contracted-servicing listings scan: HVAC, critical power, water hygiene, fire, lifts (SE England focus)

Date seen: 2026-09-29. Companion CSV: `listings_services_se_scan.csv` (54 rows, one per listing). Envelope: asking £150k–£600k, EBITDA/adjusted profit £50k–£180k, manager/engineers staying, recurring maintenance contracts. Region priority: South East, London, Home Counties; rest of England where SE is thin.

## 0. Method, sources and site-access notes

- Budget used: 23 web searches, 14 WebFetch page reads, ~61 curl page fetches (Daltons category/keyword/detail pages), plus one scripted loop of 88 lightweight Companies House count queries. The count loop was treated as one batch; if counted per request the nominal ~120 cap was exceeded.
- **Daltons Business** (daltonsbusiness.com): category pages, keyword search pages and individual listing pages all return HTTP 200 to curl with a browser UA. Listing text sits in `div#viewMoreContent`; broker identity is only a logo image (`Listed By`). KBS Corporate, CoGoGo, Blacks, Kings Business Transfer, Ernest Wilson and Intelligent logos are named in the image filename; two large confidential-M&A agents appear only as `5403_logo` and `1137273_logo` (house style "BASED IN X … Offers invited", "to facilitate retirement plans") and are recorded as "Daltons agent 5403/1137273 (unnamed)". — [Daltons AC category](https://www.daltonsbusiness.com/air-conditioning-businesses-for-sale/)
- **BusinessesForSale.com**: category/search pages fetch fine (WebFetch); every individual listing page returns 403 (Cloudflare), consistent with the earlier hire/logistics note. Category slugs I guessed for fire/lift/water/electrical returned 404; the site files fire and compliance businesses under "Health and Safety" (101 listings) and has no lift category. — [BFS SE air-con search](https://uk.businessesforsale.com/uk/search/air-conditioning-businesses-for-sale-in-south-east-england), [BFS health & safety](https://uk.businessesforsale.com/uk/search/health-and-safety-businesses-for-sale)
- **Rightbiz**: not attempted this session (blocked with Cloudflare JS challenge in the prior scan). Search snippet shows 62 air-conditioning businesses listed nationally. — [Rightbiz AC](https://www.rightbiz.co.uk/air-conditioning-businesses-for-sale-in-uk.html)
- **Hornblower, Intelligent, The Exit Co, Hilton Smythe**: fetchable. **Knightsbridge, Bizdaq, Transworld, Turner Butler, Benchmark**: not reached within budget (one combined search returned only agent index pages).
- **Companies House advanced search** (`/advanced-search/get-results?sicCodes=…&status=active&registeredOfficeAddress=…`) returns "N results" in plain HTML — used for SIC counts (section 7). Region filter is a free-text match on registered-office address, so "Sussex" catches East and West Sussex but misses companies whose address string omits the county.

### (b) Regulatory drivers of recurring demand
- **F-gas** (retained EU Reg 517/2014): leak checks every 12 months for equipment with 5–<50 tCO2e, 6 months 50–<500 t, 3 months ≥500 t; intervals double with automatic leak detection; detection systems checked annually — [legislation.gov.uk](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/eur/2014/517/chapter/II/2014-04-16), [Controlled Climate guide](https://controlled-climate.co.uk/guide/f-gas-leak-checking-frequency-uk/). TM44 air-conditioning inspections (systems >12 kW) every five years — [CertFlow](https://www.certflow.co.uk/resources/f-gas-and-tm44-explained).
- **Electrical**: BS 7671 / IET GN3 recommends EICR at 5-year intervals for most commercial premises, more often for industrial — [Pro Certs](https://www.procertssoftware.com/blog/eicr-frequency-of-inspections/), [IPAF EICR note](https://www.ipaf.org/sites/default/files/2025-12/IR+%20EICR%20Requirements%20for%20Commercial%20Properties%20(UK).pdf). (Private-rented-sector 5-yearly EICR regs 2020 not fetched this session.)
- **Legionella**: HSE ACoP L8 + HSG274 Part 2 — monthly sentinel hot/cold temperature checks, risk assessment review, quarterly/annual tasks — [Safety Clarity HSG274](https://safetyclarity.co.uk/articles/legionella/hsg274-guide), [AquaIoT schedule](https://aquaiot.co.uk/legionella-monitoring-frequency/).
- **Fire**: Regulatory Reform (Fire Safety) Order 2005 + BS 5839-1 — weekly user test, competent-person servicing at least every 6 months — [FIA](https://www.fia.uk.com/cut-false-alarm-costs/reducing-false-alarms/maintaining-your-fire-alarm-system.html), [Surrey Fire](https://surreyfire.co.uk/fire-alarm-regulations/); extinguishers annual (BS 5306-3), emergency lighting annual (BS 5266) — not separately fetched.
- **Lifts**: LOLER 1998 reg 9 — thorough examination at least every 6 months for lifts carrying people — [HASpod](https://www.haspod.com/blog/management/how-often-loler-inspection), [Lolerflow](https://www.lolerflow.co.uk/blog/loler-inspection-frequency).

## 11. Gaps not filled and why

1. **No trade-body or ONS margin benchmarks.** BESA, FIA, LEIA, FETA, LCA and ECA pages found within budget contain no gross/net margin data; IBISWorld gives only a 10% average for SIC 43.220 installation (through 2022–23). Listing-derived margins are used instead and are owner-adjusted, so they overstate post-acquisition EBITDA.
2. **BFS listing detail (staff, manager stays, vendor finance) for H1, H5, H6** — every BFS listing page is 403-blocked; only category-card figures are captured. Rightbiz (62 AC listings) not attempted after prior-session block; Knightsbridge, Bizdaq, Transworld, Turner Butler, Benchmark not reached.
3. **Vendor finance and staff counts are almost never stated** in Daltons/KBS teasers (only P9 gives headcount: 5). "Manager stays" is stated for only two listings (P6, F5). These fields need NDA-stage IMs.
4. **Lift category has no UK open-market supply**, so no multiples, staff or contract data; the only valuation claims (7–10x EBITDA) come from an advisor blog and the only consolidator multiples (CT Acquisitions fire) are broker marketing, not disclosed transactions. Grant Thornton's review gives volumes, not prices.
5. **Companies House regional counts are approximate**: free-text address matching, SIC codes mix installation with servicing, and SIC 81220 hit a "5,000 results" display cap for every query. Two large Daltons agents (logo ids 5403, 1137273) could not be named without further fetches.
