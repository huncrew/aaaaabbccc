All figures GBP, dated. ITJobsWatch figures are for the 6 months to 28 September 2026 unless stated. Where I could not find a source, the item is listed under Gaps rather than estimated. ## Key Question 1: What is the realistic gross from a 3-month, 3–4 day/week SC-cleared pre-sales AWS/AI contract in 2027 (rate × days), and the net after tax?

### Takeaway
Advertised medians for the relevant contract roles cluster at £625–£713/day (Solutions Architect £625, Presales £625, AI Architect £650, Cloud Solutions Architect £650, Presales Solution Architect £713), with 90th percentiles at £740–£821. A 13-week block at 3–4 days/week therefore grosses roughly £24k–£37k at median rates (£31k–£43k at 90th-percentile rates); net retention via a limited company outside IR35 is roughly 65–70% if the income sits on top of other higher-rate earnings (per published 2026/27 take-home tables), but higher (~80%+) if the block is the only income in the tax year.

### Inferences
- Gross for a 13-week block (my arithmetic on the cited medians): 3 days/week = 39 days; 4 days/week = 52 days. At £625/day: £24,375 (3d) / £32,500 (4d). At £650: £25,350 / £33,800. At £713: £27,807 / £37,076. At a 90th-percentile £786–£821: £30,654–£32,019 (3d) / £40,872–£42,692 (4d).
- Net: if the block is layered on top of other income already in the higher-rate band, the marginal retention through a Ltd company is roughly (1−0.19)×(1−0.3575) ≈ 52%, i.e. ~£17k net from £32.5k gross. If the block is the only income in the tax year, retention is far higher: e.g. £32,500 revenue − £12,570 salary = £19,930 profit → CT £3,787 → £16,143 dividends → dividend tax ≈£1,682 → ≈£27,000 net (~83%), before accountancy/insurance costs of ~£2k–£4.5k/yr. The published full-year tables (65–70% retention at £600–£800/day) sit between these two cases.
- The £713 Presales Solution Architect median rests on 6 postings and should be treated as indicative only; the broader Presales (£625, n=202) and Solutions Architect (£625, n=1,857) medians are the robust anchors. SC-cleared presales postings (27–39% of presales ads cite clearance) suggest the target niche is real.

## Key Question 2: What is the probability of landing such a contract within 8 weeks of starting to look, based on live-role counts and bench-time data?

### Takeaway
No source publishes a time-to-next-contract distribution for senior contractors, so a probability cannot be quantified from data; the evidence is that the volume of relevant contract adverts rose sharply in the 6 months to Sep 2026 (Solutions Architect 845→1,857; Presales 79→202; AWS 4,565→6,375; SC Cleared 4,607→9,038), but 2025 surveys showed 26% of contractors not working, only ~2 engagements per year on average, outside-IR35 share falling to 70%, and 3-month assignments now typical.

### Cited Findings
- IPSE IR35 Spotlight 2025 (n=1,039): 26% of contractors not working (21% in 2024, 18% in 2023); of those, 56% blame IR35 reform, 45% say "between roles"; 29% of over-50s not working vs 16% of 16–49s; average 2 engagements per year; 67% less confident of securing outside-IR35 roles, 24% equally, 9% more; 53% had rejected an inside-IR35 offer — [IPSE IR35 Spotlight 2025](https://www.ipse.co.uk/campaigns/ir35/ir35-spotlight-2025)
- Qdos survey (published 29 Jan 2026, >600 contractors): biggest threat to business — lack of outside-IR35 contracts 25%, finding new contract work 24%, further tax rises 22%, cost of living 9%, illness 6%, AI/automation 4%; 54% want IR35 reform reversed — [IT Contracting on Qdos survey](https://www.itcontracting.com/lack-outside-ir35-roles-concern/)
- REC/KPMG Report on Jobs: IT contractor demand index 47.0 in Dec 2025 (46.0 in Nov; 50 = growth), no growth for 29 months since Aug 2023; CV-Library contract tech postings 856 in Dec 2025 vs 813 Dec 2024 (+5%); Hays: contract tech roles "unexpectedly strong" in AI, ERP, CRM, cyber, change leadership; typical assignments three months (article 13 Jan 2026) — [Contractor UK, IT contractor demand December 2025](https://www.contractoruk.com/news/it-contractor-demand-defied-seasonal-slump-december-2025)
- VIQU IT (18 Nov 2025): clients now plan hires 6 months ahead (12 months maximum) versus 18–24 months previously; IoD confidence −74 in Sep 2025, lowest on record; "daily rates somehow don't seem to have grown in almost a decade for some temporary techies" — [Contractor UK, why UK IT contracting is now ruthless](https://www.contractoruk.com/news/why-uk-it-contracting-now-ruthless-punishing-yet-not-insurmountable)
- Live-role counts (28 Sep 2026): Solutions Architect 1,379 live contract vacancies; AI Architect 536; AI Agents 2,431; AWS 6,121; Presales Solution Architect 61; AWS Certified SA 123 — [ITJobsWatch Solutions Architect](https://www.itjobswatch.co.uk/contracts/uk/solutions%20architect.do); [AI Architect](https://www.itjobswatch.co.uk/contracts/uk/ai%20architect.do); [AI Agents](https://www.itjobswatch.co.uk/contracts/uk/agentic%20ai.do); [AWS](https://www.itjobswatch.co.uk/contracts/uk/aws.do); [Presales SA](https://www.itjobswatch.co.uk/contracts/uk/pre-sales%20solutions%20architect.do); [AWS Certified SA](https://www.itjobswatch.co.uk/contracts/uk/aws%20certified%20solutions%20architect.do)
- Kingsbridge IR35 survey: 71% of contractors want only outside-IR35 roles in the next 6–12 months, but such roles are "less than 41% of roles on offer" (page undated; sample size not given) — [Kingsbridge](https://www.kingsbridge.co.uk/blog/partners/what-the-ir35/contractor-outside-ir35/)

### Inferences
- With 1,379 live Solutions Architect contract ads and ~2,400 AI-agent ads on 28 Sep 2026, and a 6-month posting run-rate of roughly 300 SA adverts/month, an 8-week search window would see several hundred new SA-level adverts, but the SC-cleared + AWS + pre-sales + part-time intersection is far smaller (Presales SA: 6 postings/6 months; AWS Certified SA: 108/6 months).
- The IPSE "26% not working" and "2 engagements per year" figures imply meaningful gaps between contracts are normal; combined with 3-month typical assignments, a planning assumption of one contract per ~8–12-week search cannot be supported by data either way.
- Caution: the near-doubling of ITJobsWatch counts across nearly every skill (SC Cleared, AWS, Solutions Architect, Presales) in the same period may partly reflect a change in ITJobsWatch's source coverage rather than pure demand growth; the REC/KPMG index (still below 50 in Dec 2025) is a more conservative signal.

## Sub-topic: Inside vs outside IR35 share (2025–26) and effect on take-home

### Takeaway
The share of engagements outside IR35 fell to 70% in 2025 (from 80% in 2023 and 2024) among IPSE's surveyed contractors, while Kingsbridge reports outside-IR35 roles are under 41% of roles on offer; at £600–£800/day the outside-IR35 Ltd route yields £10.9k–£18.3k more per full year than inside IR35 PAYE.

### Cited Findings
- Outside-IR35 engagements: 70% private sector in 2025 (80% in 2024 and 2023); public sector 88% in 2025 (75% prior); 43% received a Status Determination Statement; 63% will only work outside IR35; 22% would accept any status — [IPSE IR35 Spotlight 2025](https://www.ipse.co.uk/campaigns/ir35/ir35-spotlight-2025)
- Outside-IR35 roles "less than 41% of roles on offer"; 71% of contractors want outside-only — [Kingsbridge](https://www.kingsbridge.co.uk/blog/partners/what-the-ir35/contractor-outside-ir35/)
- Qdos Jan 2026: 25% say lack of outside-IR35 work is the biggest threat — [IT Contracting](https://www.itcontracting.com/lack-outside-ir35-roles-concern/)
- Take-home advantage outside vs inside, 2026/27, 220 days: £600/day +£10,859; £700/day +£14,601; £800/day +£18,302 — [TaxDCal](https://www.taxdcal.co.uk/blog/inside-vs-outside-ir35-take-home-comparison)
- From April 2026 the small-company thresholds rose (£15m turnover / £7.5m balance sheet), moving IR35 status responsibility to the contractor's own company for more clients; inside IR35 the 15% employer NI is "typically absorbed within the assignment rate" — [CloudComputingJobs UK 2026 guide](https://cloudcomputingjobs.co.uk/career-advice/cloud-computing-jobs-uk-2026-contractor-day-rates-ir35)

### Inferences
- The IPSE 70% figure measures engagements contractors actually took (self-selected outside-IR35 seekers); the Kingsbridge <41% measures roles advertised. For a job-seeker, the advertised share is the relevant one: assume well under half of relevant adverts are outside IR35.

### Gaps

## Sub-topic: Umbrella / IR35 rule changes from April 2026

### Takeaway
From 6 April 2026 recruitment agencies (or the end client where there is no UK agency) are jointly and severally liable with umbrella companies for unpaid PAYE/NIC; the change targets umbrella non-compliance and does not alter outside-IR35 Ltd-company taxation, but is expected to shrink agency umbrella panels and complicate rate negotiations.

### Cited Findings
- Effective 6 April 2026; new Chapter 11 of Part 2 ITEPA 2003 plus NIC secondary legislation; HMRC policy paper "Umbrella company market – changes to Income Tax rules to tackle non-compliance" (Nov 2025); agency is primary liable party, end client liable if no agency or agency is offshore/connected; regulatory oversight of umbrellas "pencilled in for 2027"; possible effects — fewer umbrella options as agencies rationalise panels, and rate negotiations "more complicated" (article 2 Dec 2025) — [IT Contracting](https://www.itcontracting.com/agencies-umbrella-risk-april-2026/)
- Grant Thornton (2 Apr 2026): umbrella and "relevant party" jointly and severally liable for PAYE; definition covers "any business supplying labour under a contract of employment" with anti-avoidance for umbrella-like arrangements — [Grant Thornton](https://www.grantthornton.co.uk/insights/umbrella-company-reforms-2026-what-businesses-need-to-know/)
- Liability is absolute; the IR35-style "reasonable care" defence does not apply — [Hill Dickinson](https://www.hilldickinson.com/our-view/articles/umbrella-companies-hmrc-guidance-on-new-rules-from-6-april-2026-clarifies-when-end-user-clients-may-be-liable-to-operate-paye); [Albert Goodman](https://albertgoodman.co.uk/insights/implications-of-the-new-joint-and-several-liability-rules-for-umbrella-companies)
- Dividend tax rates rose 2 points from 6 April 2026 (basic 10.75%, higher 35.75%) — [Contractor UK](https://www.contractoruk.com/news/dividends-in-2026-27-an-explainer-for-contractors)

### Inferences
- For an outside-IR35 Ltd contractor the April 2026 umbrella rules are neutral; the material 2026 change is the +2pt dividend tax, which reduces Ltd take-home by roughly 2% of dividends drawn.
