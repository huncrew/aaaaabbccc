### Inferences
- In hire, "no staff" plus a six-figure asking price is common (MEWP, grab hire): the buyer is purchasing kit plus the owner's operator labour, so these should be treated as owner-technician businesses despite the B2B customer base.
- Cleaning/compliance listings that do state a role describe supervisory or oversight work (managers, supervisors, remote operation), consistent with labour-managed rather than owner-delivered service — but the majority simply do not say.
- Courier fleets built on a single network (DPD) carry concentration risk that the listing frames as "contracts in place".

### Gaps
- Owner role is "not stated" in 35 of 50 priced rows; the split between selling-led and technical owners cannot be quantified from portal text and would need broker information memoranda.
- Staff counts (even "no staff") are stated in only 11 of 50 priced rows.
