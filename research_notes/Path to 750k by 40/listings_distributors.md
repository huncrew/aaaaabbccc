Rightbiz returned HTTP 403 on every page tried; BusinessesForSale.com returned 403 on every individual listing page (category pages worked); KBS Corporate and Knightsbridge listing URLs returned 404; Blacks Business Brokers and Bizdaq (mybizdaq.com) returned HTTP 500; Turner Butler returned 403; Daltons pagination beyond page 1 was not reachable (page-2 URLs returned page 1). Business Sale Report (BSR) masks profit figures on public pages (e.g. "£16****"); masked figures are recorded as masked, not estimated. Food/hospitality, consumer retail, single-marketplace stores and franchises were excluded. Every number below is taken from the cited listing page as fetched on 28 Sep 2026.

Definitions used: Tier A = asking price stated and within (or within £1k of) £150k–£600k. Tier B = "Offers invited" but a profit figure is stated (so an EV in range is plausible, but not confirmed). Tier C = out of range, blocked detail page, or reference-only.

## Key question 1: How many distributor/wholesaler businesses at £200–450k enterprise value are actually on the market in the UK right now, and where (region)?

### Takeaway
Only a thin slice of the market is both (a) a technical/industrial/trade B2B distributor and (b) priced £150k–£600k with a public asking price: 14 listings met that test across the fetchable sources (Tier A), of which 9 sit in the £195k–£450k band. A further 17 relevant listings are "offers invited" with a stated profit figure (Tier B). Headline category counts (hundreds to >1,600 per site) are dominated by food, franchises, property and consumer goods and vastly overstate the relevant pool. Regionally the priced Tier A set skews to the South East / London (4 of 14) and East Midlands/East of England (4 of 14); many broker-led (KBS) listings do not disclose region at all.
