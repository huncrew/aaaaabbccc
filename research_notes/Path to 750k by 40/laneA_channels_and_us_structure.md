# Lane A: sales channels for personalised wall art and how to set up a proper US presence

Date: 2026-10-30. Budget used: about 78 of about 80 searches/fetches. Stopped there and wrote up.
Companion files: `laneA_personalised_candidates.md` / `.csv` (product lines, US keyword demand, Etsy median prices, POD costs). Where this file mentions "typical price points", it means those RankHero/Etsy medians unless a figure is given here.

**Seller profile assumed (from brief):** UK-resident individual. Two established Etsy shops (~8,000 sales) with the public location set to the US. US print partners and a Philippines design team. Some metal-foil designs can only be made in the UK. UK sales have earned little (lower prices, VAT). One earlier Etsy shop was cancelled by Etsy. The seller "theoretically" has access to one or two US bank accounts.

**Source labels.** **[OFFICIAL]** = the platform's or government's own page. **[3P]** = third-party commentary such as blogs, fee guides, law-firm notes or forums. **[SNIPPET]** = text attributed to an official page in search-engine results that a direct fetch did not show. Every source was accessed 2026-10-30 unless another date is given. Publication dates are shown where the page has one.

**Not tax or legal advice.** Part 2 (3) and (4) must be confirmed by a UK chartered tax adviser with US experience and, for US filings, a US preparer.

## PART 1: Channels

### 1.1 Etsy: existing US-facing shops, and a possible UK-focused shop

- **Fees, UK-based seller [3P, consistent across sources]:**
  - Transaction fee 6.5% of the order total, including postage.
  - Payment processing 4% + £0.20.
  - Regulatory operating fee rose from 0.32% to 0.48% on 22 June 2026.
  - Listing fee $0.20.
  - 20% UK VAT is charged on Etsy's fees.
  - Offsite Ads 15%, or 12% for shops over $10k a year.
  - Sources: https://channelx.world/2026/04/etsy-increase-uk-regulatory-operating-fee-2/ ; https://beancount.io/blog/2026/08/22/etsy-regulatory-operating-fee-increase-june-2026-seller-payout-bookkeeping-guide ; https://checkoutpage.com/blog/etsy-fees
- **Production partners are allowed.** Etsy's Seller Policy, quoted in search text, says "If you work with a production partner, you must disclose that production partner in your relevant listings." The Creativity Standards add that sellers must "provide accurate information about where the item will ship from" [SNIPPET of https://www.etsy.com/legal/sellers/ and https://www.etsy.com/legal/creativity]. Etsy's help article says print-on-demand partners are allowed if the design is the seller's own or the buyer's customisation [OFFICIAL, https://help.etsy.com/hc/en-us/articles/23948763872151-Does-Etsy-Allow-Drop-Shipping-or-Reselling].
  - **Relevance:** a public US location is defensible for US-printed lines. **UK-made foil items must show the UK as the ship-from location.**
- **Who buys:** Gift buyers worldwide, with the US as the core market. Personalisation is native: Etsy has a built-in personalisation field.
- **A UK-focused shop** would be a new shop under the same identity (see Part 2.1). It would mainly carry the UK-only foil designs, priced for UK gifting.
  - The brief says UK sales historically made little money. A separate shop does not fix that. Only pricing, product mix (foil = a premium product) and channel (NOTHS, below) can.
- **Fit:** (i) US lines: **core**, already proven. (ii) UK foil: good, and Etsy UK search shows UK-origin listings to UK buyers. Margin is the issue, not reach.

### 1.2 Not On The High Street (NOTHS), UK

- **Fees:**
  - A one-off joining fee of **£199 + VAT** and **25% + VAT commission** on all sales, according to the official "why sell with us" page as shown in search results [SNIPPET, https://www.notonthehighstreet.com/pages/why-sell-with-us]. A direct fetch of that page did not show the figures. The £199 is corroborated by https://www.yodeldirect.co.uk/content/how-to-sell-on-not-on-the-highstreet [3P].
  - The official FAQ says "we have a one-off joining fee" and no annual renewal fee [OFFICIAL, https://www.notonthehighstreet.com/join/faqs].
  - The seller T&Cs say commission is "calculated as a percentage of the value of a Product, including the delivery charges" (cl. 16D.i).
  - Refund fee: 2.5% of refunds + VAT (cl. 16G.ii).
  - NOTHS may add a subscription fee on 30 days' notice (cl. 16E.i) [OFFICIAL, https://www.notonthehighstreet.com/about/seller-terms-and-conditions, undated].
  - A 2014-era forum post says NOTHS has offered several fee and commission packages [3P, https://community.etsy.com/t5/Crafty-Fox-Team/Selling-on-NOTHS/td-p/51748160]. Confirm the current package with the membership team.
- **Who can sell:** "your business must be registered and trade from the UK or Northern Ireland" [OFFICIAL FAQ]. The T&Cs require a business "incorporated and/or established" in the UK with "a trading (operating) address in the United Kingdom" (cl. 4.A.i).
- **Curation:** "We're a curated marketplace who hand-select each and every one of the small brands" [OFFICIAL, why-sell-with-us page]. You apply with product images and wait at least 7 days for a response [OFFICIAL FAQ]. The stated criteria are "Products to delight", "Crowd-pleasing gifts", "Ambition", "Great value", "5-star service" [OFFICIAL, https://www.notonthehighstreet.com/join/why].
- **"Made in UK" expectation:**
  - No explicit rule was found in the visible T&Cs. They ban "replica or design copies" (cl. 11.C.i.c).
  - A forum recollection says the ethos is "designed and made by the sellers" [3P, https://www.mumsnet.com/talk/work/2753235-Notonthehighstreet-any-sellers-out-there].
  - **Gap:** whether US-printed POD fulfilment is acceptable. The UK foil designs plainly fit.
- **Fulfilment:** Seller-fulfilled. Orders must be accepted "within a maximum of two (2) Working Days" (cl. 6.B.ii) and delivered within the advertised timelines. Personalised items must be labelled as such (cl. 4.F.iii.a) [OFFICIAL T&Cs].
- **Buyers:** UK gift buyers. NOTHS claims "100 million annual views" [OFFICIAL, /join/why]. Personalised gifts are a headline category.
- **Price points:** Not collected in this pass (gap).
- **Fit:** (i) US lines: poor, because UK buyers and a 25% commission make US printing and shipping uneconomic. (ii) UK foil: **best UK fit**. It is curated, premium and personalisation-led, and the seller meets the UK-base rule.

### 1.3 Amazon: Amazon Custom, Handmade, Amazon.com and Amazon.co.uk

- **Amazon Custom (US) [OFFICIAL, https://sell.amazon.com/programs/custom]:**
  - "You must be a Professional seller in order to use Amazon Custom." The Professional plan is $39.99/month plus selling fees.
  - "All orders must be fulfilled by the seller using Fulfilled by Merchant", so FBA is not allowed.
  - Personalisation tools: Personalize (text or image upload), Configure and Assemble. Up to 5 surfaces and 15 customisations per surface.
  - Custom products are non-returnable unless damaged or materially different.
- **Amazon Handmade US [OFFICIAL, https://sell.amazon.com/programs/handmade]:**
  - Referral fee "15% or $0.30, whichever is greater". The monthly Professional fee is waived after the first month for approved Handmade sellers.
  - Entry is by application and audit.
  - Qualifying types include "Hand-designed: Original design, like an art print or stationery" and "Hand-altered: … a t-shirt or mug with a graphic".
  - **Gap:** the page does not say whether third-party production partners or POD are allowed.
- **Amazon Handmade UK [OFFICIAL, https://sell.amazon.co.uk/programmes/handmade]:**
  - "12.24% referral fee". £25/month ex-VAT is waived for approved applicants after the first month.
  - Handmade is "only available for products that are handmade, hand-altered, or hand-assembled by the artisan."
  - Personalised and made-to-order products are supported.
- **UK entity selling on Amazon.com:** Amazon lets non-US sellers list on .com. Proceeds can be paid to a local bank via "Amazon Currency Converter for Sellers": add the bank on the Deposit Methods page, and funds take 1 to 5 business days [OFFICIAL, https://sell.amazon.com/programs/amazon-currency-converter]. Third-party sources put the conversion cost at about 3.5% [3P, https://pickfu.com/blog/amazon-currency-conversion]. **A US LLC is not needed to sell on Amazon.com.**
- **Fit:** (i) US lines: **strong**. FBM suits US POD partners, and Custom matches the personalisation workflow. (ii) UK foil: good on Amazon.co.uk Handmade, if the application is approved.

### 1.4 Walmart Marketplace (UK sellers programme)

- **Qualifications [OFFICIAL, https://marketplace.walmart.com/wmp-uk-sellers/]:**
  - "Unique Taxpayer Number (UTR), Corporate Registration Number (CRN), and Form W-8BEN-E". This means a UK **company** is needed, not a sole trader.
  - "History of marketplace or eCommerce success".
  - "GTIN/UPC GS1 Company Prefix Number".
  - "B2C US warehouse or an approved 3PL fulfillment provider with shipping and return capability". The page also says "a US return warehouse is required".
  - "Zero monthly or setup fees".
- **Referral fee:** Walmart's guide lists Home, Kitchen, Decor & Garden at 15% [OFFICIAL via search, https://marketplacelearn.walmart.com/guides/Policies%20&%20standards/Product%20listings/Referral-fee-schedule-for-contract-categories]. A July 2026 article reports cuts in 14 categories from 22 June 2026, with home goods among the largest, but gives no new rate [3P, https://beancount.io/blog/2026/07/26/walmart-marketplace-referral-fee-cuts-seller-guide]. Re-check the current rate.
- **Personalisation support:** Not confirmed (gap).
- **Fit:** (i) US lines: possible, but only if a US print partner or 3PL can act as the US shipping and returns location, and the seller trades through a UK Ltd with GS1 barcodes. (ii) UK foil: poor, because of transatlantic shipping times and the returns rule.

### 1.5 eBay US and UK

- **eBay UK:**
  - Private sellers have paid no final value fee since 2024.
  - For business sellers, the per-order fee on items over £10 rose from £0.30 to £0.40 from 12 Feb 2026 [3P, https://www.valueaddedresource.net/ebay-raises-final-value-fees-in-uk-germany-2026/, 14 Jan 2026].
  - Business final value fee for Art is about 10.9% ex-VAT, with VAT added on fees [3P calculators, e.g. https://www.size.ly/tools/ebay-fee-calculator-uk].
- **eBay US:** about 13.6% final value fee for most categories plus $0.30/$0.40 per order; 1.65% international fee [3P, https://webgility.com/blog/ebay-fees]. The official page was blocked.
- **Fit:** Low for both lines. eBay is a value and secondhand marketplace with weak tools for personalised made-to-order items, and no personalisation support was verified (gap). At most a low-effort cross-list.

### 1.6 TikTok Shop UK and US

- **UK [OFFICIAL, UK Seller Registration Policy, page dated 08/09/2026, https://seller-uk.tiktok.com/university/essay?knowledge_id=7753847099606786&default_language=en-GB]:**
  - Sole traders and companies may register.
  - Both must "Store their inventory in the country of registration".
  - Bank must be UK or EU. Company accounts must match the entity name; sole-trader accounts must match the ID.
  - Standard commission is 9% including VAT [OFFICIAL via search, https://seller-uk.tiktok.com/university/essay?knowledge_id=7753824408913665&default_language=en-GB].
  - A cross-border UK–EU pilot started 21 Sep 2026, with wider access planned from 19 Oct 2026 [3P news, https://www.equalocean.com/news/2026092122208-tiktok-shop-plans-uk-eu-cross-border-expansion-wider-access-scheduled-october].
- **US:**
  - Individuals must live in the US and hold US ID.
  - Business entities need a US business address, an EIN, and a primary representative with US ID and an SSN or ITIN [OFFICIAL via search, https://seller-us.tiktok.com/university/essay?knowledge_id=6837894379964161&lang=en and https://seller-us.tiktok.com/university/essay?knowledge_id=7750756652844842].
  - Consultancies say non-residents need a separate US representative [3P, TikTok videos].
  - **A UK resident cannot meet the US rules without a genuine US representative. Do not use a nominee.**
- **Fit:** (i) US lines: not available without a real US operating partner. (ii) UK foil: possible on TikTok Shop UK, because inventory and production are in the UK. It needs video content and possibly creator affiliates. Personalisation tooling was not verified (gap).

### 1.7 Faire (wholesale: US, UK, EU)

- **Commission:**
  - Faire's own help article, as summarised in search results, says brands outside North America pay the 15% commission plus a one-time 10% referral fee on a new retailer's first order (25% total), then 15% on reorders [SNIPPET, https://faire.com/support/articles/360040446591].
  - Since 5 July 2023, North American brands pay 15% plus a $10 new-customer fee [OFFICIAL blog via search, https://blog.faire.com/selling/north-america-pricing-update/].
  - Payment processing runs from 1.9% + $0.30 (60-day payout) to 3.5% + $0.30 (next day) [3P, https://stocksmith.io/blog/how-much-does-faire-charge].
  - Faire Direct (retailers you bring yourself) is 0% commission [3P].
- **Buyers:** Independent retailers in the US, Canada, UK, EU and Australia.
- **Fit:** Weak for personalised items, because retailers buy stock. Moderate for non-personalised foil prints sold to UK and EU gift shops. Wholesale is roughly 50% of retail, so margin must survive that plus 25% (see companion file).

### 1.8 Zazzle, Redbubble, Society6, Spreadshirt (royalty POD marketplaces)

- **Zazzle [OFFICIAL, https://www.zazzle.co.nz/hc/article/ambassador_referral_commissions_creator_royalties-30810357489175, table effective 1 Apr 2025]:**
  - Physical products: 5% standard royalty, 50% maximum.
  - A "Marketing Royalty Fee" of 35–50% of gross royalty applies by department.
  - Supports customisable (personalised) templates; payouts by PayPal or cheque.
  - **The only one of the four with real personalisation.**
- **Redbubble [OFFICIAL via search, https://help.redbubble.com/hc/en-us/articles/50959863016724 ; blog Aug 2025 https://blog.redbubble.com/2025/08/artist-account-tiers-and-fees/]:**
  - Platform fee: Standard 50%, Premium 20%, Pro 0%, capped at $150 per period.
  - An excess-markup fee of 50% applies on markups above 20%.
  - No personalisation.
- **Society6 [3P only]:** Reportedly Free / Basic $4.99 / Pro $12.99 plans, with the Free plan taking 10% [3P, https://www.topbubbleindex.com/blog/society6-fees-explained-artist-earnings/]. Unverified.
- **Spreadshirt [3P]:** Fixed design commission on the marketplace; Spreadshop uses own pricing. An older source gives a 20% affiliate rate. A June 2026 German review says designer earnings have fallen [3P, search summary]. Apparel-led.
- **Fit:** Low-margin passive income only. Prices and production are set by the platform, and the UK foil items cannot be made there. Zazzle is the only one worth a test, for personalised wall art and invitations.

- Wayfair's checklist asks for "Dropship capability from a warehouse in your selling region", legal business IDs, and banking and tax details for that region [OFFICIAL, https://sell.wayfair.com/onboarding-checklist].

### 1.11 Own Shopify site

## PART 2: A proper US presence

### 2.1 Etsy's rules: shop country, bank, identity, multiple shops, previous cancellations

1. **Shop country is fixed at opening.**
   - "Select the country you'll run your shop from." Shops can only open "in a country where Etsy Payments is available." [OFFICIAL, https://help.etsy.com/hc/articles/115015672808]. The article gives no way to change the country later.
   - An Etsy search summary and community moderators say: "generally speaking, you cannot change the country your shop is based in and all verification / banking information must be based on the country you used when you set up your shop." Relocating outside the eurozone "typically" requires a new shop [3P/community, https://community.etsy.com/t5/Technical-Issues/Assistance-Needed-Relocation-and-Bank-Account-Change-Policy/td-p/146511316 ; https://www.alura.io/academy/update-your-etsy-shop-location].
   - **Implication: the existing shops cannot be converted into US-country shops.** A US-country shop would be a new shop.
2. **Public location is not the same as finance country.** The brief says the shops' *public* location is set to the US. Etsy separately holds the finance/bank country on the Legal and tax information page. The Creativity Standards require accurate ship-from information (1.1). US-printed items shipping from the US are consistent with that. UK-made foil items must show the UK as the origin.
3. **Identity and bank name must match.**
   - "The ID must not be expired and needs to match the name on the bank account you added to get paid" [OFFICIAL, https://help.etsy.com/hc/articles/115015672808].
   - "The full name you enter must match your shop's bank account information … If the bank account name is a business … enter the full business name along with a business entity" [OFFICIAL, https://help.etsy.com/hc/en-us/articles/360000344508].
   - Incorporated businesses must verify "business information in addition to your personal identity". Owners of 25% or more ("UBOs") must be listed [OFFICIAL, https://help.etsy.com/hc/en-us/articles/360001980067-How-to-Verify-Your-Seller-Information-for-Etsy-Payments].
4. **Tax identity is linked across shops.**
   - "Etsy requires using the same taxpayer information (Name, Address, and SSN/EIN) on each of your shops."
   - A US address or USD payouts make you a "US seller" for 1099-K purposes unless a W-8BEN is filed [OFFICIAL, https://help.etsy.com/hc/articles/360000336447].
5. **Multiple shops are allowed, with disclosure.**
   - "You're welcome to run multiple shops at once on Etsy." Each needs its own account and email, and "you must list all the shops you run in the Public Profile section of each account" [OFFICIAL, https://help.etsy.com/hc/en-us/articles/360017604474].
6. **After suspension or cancellation:**
   - Etsy's Terms (fetch blocked) are quoted by third parties as: Etsy may suspend "your account (and any accounts Etsy determines are related to your account)".
   - The Oct 2025 Payments Policy update extends holds to "any accounts Etsy determines are related to your account" [3P quoting policy, https://www.valueaddedresource.net/etsy-payment-policy-update-october-2025/, 30 Aug 2025].
   - Seller guides attribute to Etsy: if "permanently suspended … you're not allowed to buy or sell on Etsy, or create a new account to buy or sell on Etsy" [3P, https://www.growingyourcraft.com/blog/etsy-shop-account-suspension-ban]. **Not verified on an Etsy page (blocked).**
   - Etsy has an appeal route: "How to File an Appeal for a Permanently Suspended Account" (linked from the help article above).
   - **Implication:**
     - Any new shop by the same person, whether as an individual or through an LLC where they are the UBO, will be linked by Etsy to the existing shops and to the cancelled one.
     - Whether this is a problem depends on why the earlier shop was cancelled, and whether it was a *permanent* suspension of the person or a closure of one shop.
     - The two current shops operating normally suggests the person is not banned. **Confirm this with Etsy Support in writing before opening any further shop.**

### 2.2 The legitimate route to a US-based shop: a US LLC owned by a UK resident

**Steps (each must be in the seller's own name or the LLC's name):**

1. **Form the LLC.**
   - **Wyoming:** $100 filing, $60/year annual report (higher if WY assets exceed $250k). A registered agent is required, about $100–$300/year, commonly about $125 [3P, https://www.llcuniversity.com/wyoming-llc/costs/, modified 10 Jun 2026].
   - **Delaware:** LLCs "must pay the $400 yearly tax on or before June 1st". Late payment costs a $200 penalty plus 1.5%/month [OFFICIAL, https://corp.delaware.gov/alt-entitytaxinstructions/]. A registered agent is also required (cost not collected).
   - **New Mexico:** about $50 formation and no annual report [3P, https://howtostartanllc.com/cost-to-form-an-llc/llc-annual-fees-by-state]. Not confirmed with the NM Secretary of State.
2. **Get an EIN (free).**
   - "You never have to pay a fee for an EIN." Online application is not available if "Your principal place of business is outside the U.S." [OFFICIAL, https://www.irs.gov/businesses/small-businesses-self-employed/get-an-employer-identification-number].
   - "Nominees can't apply for an EIN and shouldn't be listed on Form SS-4." The responsible party is the person who "owns, controls or exercises effective control" [OFFICIAL, https://www.irs.gov/businesses/small-businesses-self-employed/responsible-parties-and-nominees].
   - Non-US applicants use phone, fax or mail with Form SS-4.
3. **Open a US business bank account in the LLC's name.**
   - **Mercury:** accepts US-formed companies with non-US founders. Eligibility depends on country of residence, and the prohibited list did not name the UK in the search summary. Registered-agent addresses are not accepted as the business address. A pending EIN is allowed with a completed SS-4 [SNIPPET of support.mercury.com eligibility and prohibited-countries pages].
   - **Relay:** "accepts U.S.-registered businesses owned by non-U.S. citizens or non-U.S. residents. However, the business must have an operating presence in the U.S." It needs a passport and a physical US address [SNIPPET of https://support.relayfi.com/hc/en-us/articles/38049251551636].
   - **Wise Business:** "Non-US citizens can open a US Wise Business account with a registered US business and US operating address" [OFFICIAL blog, https://wise.com/us/blog/llc-for-non-us-residents].
   - **Practical blocker:** all three expect some US address or operating presence. A registered-agent address alone may not be enough (gap).
4. **Annual US filing.**
   - A foreign-owned single-member LLC is a "foreign-owned U.S. DE" that must "file a pro forma Form 1120 … with Form 5472 attached" by the 1120 due date.
   - Reportable transactions include "contributions to, and distributions from, the entity".
   - Penalty: "$25,000 … on any reporting corporation that fails to file Form 5472 when due" [OFFICIAL, https://www.irs.gov/instructions/i5472, rev. 12/2024].
   - This applies even with no US tax due [3P, https://1040abroad.com/blog/foreign-owned-llc-reporting-and-taxes-explained/].
5. **Estimated yearly running cost (US side only):**
   - State fee: WY $60, DE $400, NM $0.
   - Registered agent: about $100–$300.
   - 5472/1120 preparer: not sourced. Typical fees in the market are a few hundred dollars (unverified, so get quotes).
   - Bank: Mercury, Relay and Wise base tiers are reportedly free (not verified on official pages).
   - **Total:** roughly $300–$1,200 a year before UK accountancy costs. That is an estimate, not a sourced figure.
6. **What the LLC route does and does not unlock:**
   - It does **not** convert the existing shops, because the country is fixed (2.1).
   - It does **not** hide the owner: the seller would be the declared UBO and identity-verified.
   - It allows a *new* US-country Etsy shop with USD payouts.
   - It is not needed for Amazon.com.
   - It does not by itself satisfy TikTok Shop US (needs a US representative with SSN/ITIN) or Walmart (whose UK route uses a UK CRN anyway).
   - **Its commercial benefit is mainly USD banking and a cleaner US paper trail, not access to buyers.**

### 2.3 UK tax treatment of a US LLC for a UK resident (summary; an accountant must confirm)

- **HMRC's practice:**
  - In Revenue & Customs Brief 15/2015, HMRC "will continue with its existing practice to treat US Limited Liability Companies (LLCs) as companies" [OFFICIAL, https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/revenue-and-customs-brief-15-2015-hmrc-response-to-the-supreme-court-decision-in-george-anson-v-hmrc-2015-uksc-44, 25 Sep 2015].
  - That means opaque for UK purposes, even though the US disregards a single-member LLC.
- ***Anson v HMRC* [2015] UKSC 44:**
  - A UK-resident member of a Delaware LLC won double-tax credit for US tax on his share of profits. The outcome turned on the facts of the LLC agreement and Delaware law.
  - HMRC treats it as fact-specific. Its December 2023 guidance says *Anson* is not binding in later cases.
  - For individuals, the combined US and UK burden can exceed 60% when relief is denied [3P, https://www.boodlehatfield.com/?p=10824, 15 May 2026 ; https://www.pkf-l.com/insights/tax-talk-doing-business-in-the-us-through-an-llc-uk-tax-pitfalls/, 16 Dec 2021].
- **Central management and control:**
  - Taylor Wessing (fetch blocked) is quoted in search results: where the LLC "is being managed and controlled by UK-resident individuals, HMRC may regard it as a UK-resident company that is chargeable to corporation tax" [SNIPPET of 3P, https://www.taylorwessing.com/en/insights-and-events/insights/2024/passle/llcs-and-uk-tax---a-trap-for-the-unwary, 19 Mar 2024].
  - **This is the central risk for a one-person LLC run from the UK.** It can create UK corporation tax, a company tax return, and dividend tax on what the seller draws out, on top of the US 5472 filing.
- **2026 consultation:**
  - The UK government opened a consultation (closing 31 July 2026) on legislation to treat US LLCs as transparent for **individual** members where the LLC is transparent in the US **and is not UK tax-resident and has no UK permanent establishment**.
  - Single-member trading LLCs would then be treated as sole traders. Nothing has been enacted [3P, https://www.macfarlanes.com/insights/102n2df/uk-government-launches-consultation-on-solving-the-llc-double-taxation-problem, 12 Jun 2026].
  - A UK-run LLC may still fail the "not UK resident" condition.
- **US side:** A disregarded LLC owned by a non-resident generally pays US federal income tax only if it is engaged in a US trade or business, which is fact-dependent [3P, https://www.bench.co/blog/tax-tips/tax-implications-foreign-owned-business-us]. **Whether ongoing use of US print partners creates a US trade or business, and how the UK–US treaty permanent-establishment test applies, was not resolved here (gap).**
- **Bottom line:** for a UK-resident sole owner, a US LLC usually *adds* compliance on both sides of the Atlantic without lowering UK tax. Confirm with a UK–US cross-border adviser before forming one.

### 2.4 US sales tax when selling only through marketplaces

- **Etsy:** "is a registered sales tax vendor in the states listed" and "No action is required on your part to collect sales tax from buyers"; "Sellers can't opt out" [OFFICIAL, https://help.etsy.com/hc/articles/360000343968].
- **All sales-tax states:** marketplace facilitators such as Amazon, eBay and Etsy are required by all states that have a sales tax to collect for sellers [3P, https://wipfli.com/insights/articles/tax-marketplace-facilitators-and-sales-tax-what-you-need-to-know].
- **Exceptions where registration may still be needed:**
  - **Missouri:** sellers "may still be required to register with the state and report all Missouri sales".
  - **Nebraska:** sellers "must still register with the state and report all Nebraska sales".
  - Some states expect zero-dollar returns from registered sellers [3P, https://www.avalara.com/us/en/learn/guides/state-by-state-guide-to-marketplace-facilitator-laws.html, updated 31 Jan 2025 ; https://craftybase.com/blog/etsy-marketplace-sales-taxes].
- **Practical reading:**
  - Selling *only* through Etsy, Amazon and Walmart generally means no state registration. The exceptions are Missouri and Nebraska, plus any state where marketplace sales count towards the seller's own threshold.
  - **Own-site (Shopify) US sales are not covered**, and state economic-nexus thresholds (commonly $100k) would apply.
  - Confirm for MO and NE with a US sales-tax adviser.
- This is the same whether the seller is a UK individual or a US LLC.

### 2.5 Risks of a US bank account not in the seller's own name or their own entity's name

- **Breaks Etsy's matching rules.** The bank name must match the name or business entity on Legal and tax information, and that must match the verified ID [OFFICIAL, 2.1 point 3]. Linking someone else's account means misrepresenting who the seller is.
- **Tax reporting goes to the wrong person.** USD payouts and US details trigger 1099-K reporting against a TIN. The same taxpayer information must be used across all shops [OFFICIAL, https://help.etsy.com/hc/articles/360000336447]. Income would be reported under another person's or entity's TIN while the UK seller owes UK tax on it.
- **Holds and suspension.** Payment providers name "bank account holder name differs from seller profile" as a standard reason to pause payouts, and AML monitoring looks at fund flows [3P, https://stripe.com/resources/more/marketplace-compliance-responsibilities].
  - Etsy can act on "related accounts" [3P quoting policy, 2.1 point 6].
  - With one shop already cancelled, a mismatch could put the **two live shops** at risk.
- **Nominee arrangements are explicitly barred at the IRS level** for EINs [OFFICIAL, 2.2 step 2]. Bank terms generally restrict accounts to the holder's own business (bank T&Cs not fetched; gap).
- **Legal exposure** for the account holder (taxable receipts that are not theirs) and for the seller (no legal claim to funds held by a third party).
- **Neutral conclusion:** the only compliant uses of a US bank account are (a) an account in the seller's own name, to the extent a platform allows a foreign-country shop to use it (Etsy generally does not for a UK-country shop), or (b) an account in the name of the seller's own US entity, used for a shop registered to that entity.
